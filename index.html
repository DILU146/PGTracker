ABOUT YOUR JOURNEY

Pregnancy, one beautiful week at a time. 🌸

Pregnancy Journey is a simple and informative pregnancy-tracking app designed to help expectant mothers understand their pregnancy week by week.

Enter your Last Menstrual Period (LMP) date and the app automatically calculates your estimated due date, current pregnancy week, and pregnancy progress.

Each week brings easy-to-understand information about baby development, approximate baby size, common pregnancy symptoms, helpful tips, and activities for your journey.
