About Pregnancy Journey 🌸


Our Pregnancy Journey App is a simple and informative pregnancy-tracking app
designed to help expectant mothers understand their pregnancy week by week.
Users can enter their Last Menstrual Period (LMP) date, and the app
automatically calculates the estimated due date, current pregnancy week, and
pregnancy progress. Each week provides easy-to-understand information about baby development,
approximate baby size, common pregnancy symptoms, and helpful tips. Future weeks
remain locked and become available as the pregnancy progresses, creating a
personalized 40-week journey. Our goal is to make pregnancy information simple,
organized, accessible, and easy to understand, helping users follow their journey one week at a time.
